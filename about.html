WHO WE ARE

PRINCIPAL MANUFACTURER,
SYSTEMS INTEGRATOR
& TURNKEY PARTNER

Green Med Ltd. is a UK-based principal manufacturer, systems integrator and turnkey laboratory solutions provider. We design and manufacture our own product line under one brand and one standard, integrate equipment into complete laboratory environments, and deliver full-scope infrastructure — from initial feasibility and system specification through procurement, installation, commissioning, and long-term technical support.

We serve healthcare institutions, government ministries, research institutes, and international organisations across 18+ countries — managing every phase of your laboratory investment under a single contract, with a single point of accountability.

Our clients include the UN, WHO, FAO, UNICEF, UNDP, and UNOPS — organisations that demand the highest standards of delivery, transparency, and compliance.

OUR DIFFERENTIATORS

WHY
GREEN MED

We combine our own manufacturing capability, international procurement expertise, and end-to-end project management into a single, accountable delivery model.

01
Principal Manufacturer of Our Own Product Line
We own the design, technical files, brand rights and quality standards of our manufactured product line — produced through a centrally governed, multi-country network of branch facilities and authorized sub-manufacturers, all operating to the same Green Med standard.
02
Single Contract, Single Responsibility
One point of accountability from feasibility to handover. We coordinate all vendors, logistics, customs, and installation — eliminating the complexity of managing multiple contractors.
03
International Procurement Network
Access to accredited manufacturers across Europe, Asia, and North America — ensuring quality instrumentation at competitive pricing, with full traceability.
04
Regulatory & Compliance Expertise
Deep knowledge of ISO 15189, WHO guidelines, and GLP/GMP requirements — ensuring every laboratory meets the necessary accreditation and compliance benchmarks.
05
Post-Handover Technical Support
Long-term service agreements, staff training programmes, and ongoing maintenance ensure your laboratory continues to operate at peak performance long after commissioning.
06
Trusted by International Organisations
Our track record with the UN, WHO, FAO, UNICEF, UNDP, and UNOPS reflects a consistent ability to meet the demanding procurement, reporting, and delivery standards of global institutions.
CERTIFIED QUALITY SYSTEM

QUALITY & CERTIFICATIONS

As the legal manufacturer of its own product line, Green Med Ltd. designs, documents and releases its products under a certified quality management system.

ISO 13485:2016
Certified medical-device quality management system covering design and manufacture — spanning EN ISO 13485, EU MDR and IVDR.
ISO 9001:2015
A certified ISO 9001:2015 quality management system underpins Green Med's business and operational processes alongside its medical-device QMS.
UKCA / CE Marking
Products are placed on the market under Green Med Ltd. as the legal manufacturer, supported by UKCA and CE Declarations of Conformity.
MHRA Registration
Green Med Ltd. is registered with the UK Medicines & Healthcare products Regulatory Agency (MHRA) as a medical device manufacturer.
Legal Manufacturer: Green Med Ltd.
