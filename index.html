01 — PRINCIPAL MANUFACTURER
Our Position as Principal Manufacturer

Green Med Ltd. operates as a UK-based principal manufacturer. The design, technical files, brand rights and quality standards of our products rest with us. Although production takes place across multiple locations, the centre that defines the identity and the standard of every product is Green Med Ltd.

02 — MANUFACTURING NETWORK
Our Multi-Site Manufacturing Network

Our manufacturing capacity is not tied to a single geography. Through branch production facilities located in different countries and our authorized sub-manufacturers, we produce close to where demand arises. This structure shortens lead times, simplifies logistics and lets us respond faster to local needs.

03 — PARTNERSHIP MODEL
Sub-Manufacturers & Our Partnership Model

Our sub-manufacturers are authorized partners that produce to the technical specifications and quality criteria defined by Green Med Ltd. Product specification and the quality framework are set by the centre; our manufacturing partners execute production within that framework. This keeps a consistent level of quality across a wide manufacturing network.
