HOW WE PRODUCE

THE PRODUCTION PROCESS

01
Design & R&D

Every product begins as a Green Med design. Our engineering and R&D define the specification, materials and performance criteria — the intellectual property that makes us the principal manufacturer.

02
Technical File & Documentation

Each device is documented under our ISO 13485 quality management system — technical file, risk management, verification and validation records — before any production is authorised.

03
Sourcing & Incoming Inspection

Raw materials and components are procured from qualified suppliers and inspected against defined acceptance criteria on arrival, so production starts from a controlled baseline.

04
Production

Manufacturing runs at our branch production facilities and authorised sub-manufacturers — always to the specification and quality criteria set by the centre. Producing close to demand shortens lead times and simplifies logistics.

05
Quality Control & Testing

In-process and final quality control verify that each batch meets the Green Med standard. Nothing moves forward until it conforms.

06
Packaging, Labelling & Release

Products are packaged and labelled with the Green Med identity — including the legal manufacturer, batch and reference information — and released to market under our Declaration of Conformity.

CAPABILITIES

OUR MANUFACTURING NETWORK

Multi-Site Production

Branch production facilities in different countries let us produce close to where demand arises — shorter lead times, simpler logistics, faster response to local needs.

Authorised Sub-Manufacturers

Our partners produce strictly to the technical specifications and quality criteria defined by Green Med Ltd. The centre sets the standard; partners execute within that framework.

One Governed Standard

Regardless of where a product is made, its specification and quality framework are centrally governed — keeping a consistent level of quality across the whole network.

ISO 13485 Quality System

Design and manufacture of our product line operate under a certified ISO 13485 medical-device quality management system.

Legal Manufacturer of Record

CE-marked devices are placed on the market under Green Med Ltd. as the legal manufacturer, supported by a Declaration of Conformity.

Traceability

Batch and reference information travels with every product, linking each unit back to its technical file and quality records.

Legal Manufacturer: Green Med Ltd.

Green Med Ltd. is the legal manufacturer of record for its own product line. Regulatory documentation, including our ISO 13485 certificate and CE Declaration of Conformity, is available on request.
